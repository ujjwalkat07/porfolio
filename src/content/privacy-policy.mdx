# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ujjwal Katiyar ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit [https://ujjwalkatiyar.in](https://ujjwalkatiyar.in) (the "Website").

We respect your privacy and are committed to transparency. This Website is a personal developer portfolio and technical publication. We do not sell your personal data or track you across the web.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Voluntarily Provide
When you contact us using our [Contact Form](https://ujjwalkatiyar.in/contact), we collect the following details:
- **Name**
- **Email Address**
- **Subject**
- **Message Content**

**Purpose:** We collect this information solely to review, respond to, and communicate with you regarding your message or inquiry.

### B. Information Processed Automatically (Privacy-Preserving Analytics)
When you browse our Website, our servers receive standard technical connection data:
- **Unique View Counting:** To count unique impressions on our articles without tracking individual users, our server reads the incoming request IP address, immediately generates a one-way cryptographic hash (**SHA-256**), and uses that anonymous hash solely to avoid double-counting views. **We do not store raw IP addresses in our database for analytics.**
- **Network Logs:** Our edge network infrastructure processes standard technical data (such as browser type, operating system, HTTP referrers, and timestamps) strictly to prevent abuse, mitigate DDoS attacks, and maintain server reliability.

### C. Client-Side Storage (Theme Preferences)
We do not use first-party tracking cookies. We utilize your browser's **`localStorage`** strictly to save your chosen UI display preference (Light mode, Dark mode, or System default) via our theme provider so your preference persists across page visits.

### D. What We Do NOT Collect
- We **do not** collect sensitive personal information (such as government IDs, racial or ethnic origin, or religious beliefs).
- We **do not** collect GPS or precise device geolocation data.
- We **do not** have user accounts, logins, password databases, or payment/checkout systems.

## 2. How We Store and Protect Your Information

- **Database Infrastructure:** Contact form submissions and aggregated page-view counters are securely stored using **Supabase** (PostgreSQL cloud database) over encrypted TLS/SSL connections with server-side authentication keys.
- **Data Minimization:** We only retain contact messages for as long as needed to maintain our professional communication or until you request deletion.
- **Security:** We apply industry-standard security protocols, including end-to-end HTTPS encryption, Content Security Policies, and strict database access controls.

3. **Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising:**

## 5. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as under the GDPR, UK GDPR, or US State Privacy Laws like California's CCPA/CPRA), you may have the following rights:
- **Right to Access:** Request a copy of the personal information we maintain about you.
- **Right to Rectification:** Request corrections to any inaccurate or incomplete information.
- **Right to Erasure (Deletion):** Request the deletion of any contact messages or personal data you have submitted to us.
- **Right to Withdraw Consent:** Where processing is based on consent, withdraw your consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, simply email us at [contact@ujjwalkatiyar.in](mailto:contact@ujjwalkatiyar.in) or send a message via our [Contact Page](https://ujjwalkatiyar.in/contact). We will verify and fulfill your request promptly without fee or penalty.
